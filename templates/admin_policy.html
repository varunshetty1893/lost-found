A report takes the risk level that applies when it is posted or edited. Saving here does not change reports that are already published; they keep the level they were posted with.

2. Who verifies, and how the item is handed back

  • Low risk, direct handover allowed: the finder may verify the claimant from the private description, then both people confirm the exchange.
  • Medium risk, direct handover with a condition: the finder may verify only if the claim includes a photo or a detailed description (at least {{ limits.medium }} characters). Otherwise it goes to authorized staff.
  • High risk, staff verification only: authorized staff verify the claim and the item is returned through the college office. Direct handover is not offered. Items a finder marks as possibly unsafe are treated the same way.

3. Claim review and escalation

These rules apply whatever the risk level. They are built into the system and cannot be switched off.

  • More than one open claim on an item: staff decide.
  • The item is held by the college, or the finder chose to hand it to the college office: staff decide.
  • A rejected claim that is appealed: staff decide, and their decision is final.
  • The finder's account is suspended: their pending claims go to staff, and a direct handover in progress is marked disputed.
  • Both people must confirm a direct exchange. If either reports a problem, the handover is disputed and only uninvolved staff can resolve it.
  • Finder claims that nobody answered for {{ limits.stale }} days appear on the Staff Desk.
