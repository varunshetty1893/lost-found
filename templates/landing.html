How it works

From “I lost it” to “I got it back”

  1. 1

    Report or search

    Describe the item with a photo, or search the listings first. Found items can be kept safe with you or handed to the Security Office.

  2. 2

    Claim with proof

    Owners send details only they would know. The poster reviews every claim, and contact details stay hidden until one is approved.

  3. 3

    Hand over safely

    Ownership is verified first. The item is then returned in person, and the return is recorded only after the handover is confirmed.
