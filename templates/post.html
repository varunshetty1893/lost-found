{{ icon('sparkles') }} Tips for a good report

  • Use a clear name, such as “Blue Nike backpack”, instead of “bag”.
  • Add the exact place and date so matches are easy to spot.
  • Put the detail only the owner would know (a sticker, what is inside) in the private field, not in the public description.
  • Put your phone or email only in the contact box. Never write it in the description. Only a person who found your item can see it.
  • New reports are checked by staff before they appear publicly.
